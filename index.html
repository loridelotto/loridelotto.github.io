01 / 06Qiskit · Python

Quantum Option Pricing with Amplitude Estimation

Implementing quantum amplitude estimation and iterative QAE from scratch to price European and barrier options under Black-Scholes and Merton jump-diffusion. Compares encoding the full price path against loading only the terminal distribution, in qubits, circuit depth and pricing error. Project I proposed; team of 3.

In progress  TU Delft
02 / 06Python

Statistical Arbitrage & Multiple Testing

Re-implementing the Avellaneda–Lee PCA stat-arb strategy at BlackSwan Quants, and asking how much of its Sharpe ratio survives once every tested variant, fat tails and trading costs are accounted for (deflated Sharpe ratio, probability of backtest overfitting).

In progress  BlackSwan Quants↗
03 / 06Jupyter Notebook

Pairs Trading Study

Backtested a KO/PEP pairs strategy over 1990–2026 with a strict out-of-sample split, rolling cointegration tests and block bootstrap. Showed that the in-sample Sharpe of 0.45 was spurious (−0.50 out-of-sample).

Hackathon · Quant research↗

Option Pricing Engine

Three independent European option pricers built from scratch: Black-Scholes closed form with analytical Greeks, a CRR binomial tree with early exercise, and Monte Carlo with antithetic and control variates.
